# Challenge and Extend the Draft

Use the original task, complete contract, source, and working checkpoint within the remaining original budget. An additional finding does not complete the challenge. The integration cutoff below takes precedence over further investigation.

## Select a deciding question

At entry, return to the original contract and use the outcome-to-evidence trace in [Reviewing Across Owners](reviewing.md#draft-investigation) to generate questions beyond the draft's list. Select a material result for which the existing evidence does not connect source facts to the meaning used at the decision, even if no question about it is pending. If that justification is already established, reuse the evidence and select another relation; do not manufacture a defect.

Challenge completed claims and dismissals against the exact contract conditions and their actual source arguments or witness inputs. A checked consumer or one condition of a jointly qualified result does not cover the other derivations. Necessary validity repairs and required validation remain prior, but broadening a known finding does not substitute for generating questions about unexamined relations.

Choose the smallest feasible deciding step from these newly exposed relations and existing open questions. For comparable contractual risk, investigate an unexamined justification or resolve an open relation before extending an established cause. Retain concrete blockers or a substantively higher-risk reason for deferral in the existing report. After each deciding step, update its scope and select again from the contract and evidence, not only the pending list. Do not require exhaustive field or hypothesis coverage.

## Investigate and preserve the continuation

Compare the selected promise with the actual guards and fact transformations, and attempt a reachable counterexample. Use representation and access-function references to locate the implicated producers and consumers.

For a disputed cross-source dependency, establish the selected contractual condition in the baseline's source facts and observe the required result through the original consumer. Valid data that leaves that condition false is not an execution witness for the obligation. Keep those qualifying facts and other contract-relevant conditions fixed, change only the selected boundary to a supported failure or unavailable condition, and execute the same consumer from equivalent initial state. Compare the still-required result, not whole-output equality. A different condition or gate investigates a different relation. Do not demand preservation if the failed condition is contractually necessary to validity or permission.

Reuse qualifying data already established in the investigation or a supported seed when production is not disputed; apply Verification when the derivation is disputed. An absent-source failure and producer/consumer checks on different qualifying data do not establish whether valid information is suppressed. Exercise only the implicated path with safe effects, and record the compared inputs and observed outcomes in the existing checkpoint.

Take one bounded source comparison or focused check that can change the answer, then update the same question before selecting further work. State exactly what the evidence answers. When new evidence resolves a material question, check its scope against the original promise before closing the surrounding investigation. Keep the valid answer; give any newly exposed, distinct unresolved outcome its own deciding action in the same checkpoint. Do not re-prove an unchanged result merely to strengthen it. Partial or failed checks leave the unanswered relation open with its next action or concrete blocker. Changing topic, finding a related bug, or reaching the contract's end does not remove it. Lack of production execution alone does not block a safe local witness. Distinguish source-backed conclusions from executed evidence.

## Integrate the existing report

Before preparing or editing a probe, check whether authoring, execution, result inspection, and recording can fit while preserving integration. If not, choose a smaller deciding step or integrate; do not begin work that only fits by omitting execution or reporting.

Reserve the final 10% of each supplied original cap for integration; the first 90% threshold reached ends new investigation, including fixture edits. Integrate earlier when no complete deciding step fits before that boundary.

The working report is the deliverable, not material for a new report. During integration, correct inaccurate claims or missing evidence references in place, and retain every unanswered question and unmet criterion. Do not compose a replacement report or repeat it in the final response. An already adequate report needs no final rewrite.

Necessary validation may run only if execution, inspection, recording, and a short final response still fit; otherwise record it as unmet, not waived. Preserve the distinction between an executed witness and later edits. Once these minimal updates are complete, issue a short final response identifying the report, actual stopping reason, and remaining limitations. Do not spend apparent remaining time on expansion or polishing. A later first clock reading does not restart the original allocation.

Without supplied caps, integrate after traversing the original contract with no feasible material follow-up remaining. A completed traversal is not proof of conformance. Keep working notes temporary.
